How it works

From the box to the game.

  1. Step 1

    Unbox it

    Each piece comes numbered, with its USB cable.

  2. Step 2

    Plug in the base

    Your computer sees it straight away. There are no drivers to install and nothing to set up.

  3. Step 3

    Play

    The game starts from the statue in a few seconds, and your saves stay on it too.

  4. Bonus

    Add Forge, if you like

    Install Forge, our hub app, to keep all your Relics in one library. You don't need it to play.

What Relic is

Made to sit on your shelf, and to be played.

Each figure is about ten centimetres tall, printed in SLA resin and finished by hand, so small differences between pieces are normal. Its design comes from the game it carries, and the full game is built into its base.

Nothing to install

The game runs straight from the statue. If you own more than one Relic, the Forge app can keep them together on your computer.

It's really yours

There's no account and no server between you and the game. You can lend it, sell it, or keep it for as long as you like.

The game survives

The files stay readable on the statue, so playing it never depends on a store staying online.

Two apps

A light app inside the statue, a full one on your computer.

You don't need to install anything to play. When you own more than one, a companion app keeps the collection in order.

Inside the Relic

Ember

Lightweight app living in the Flux partition. It boots straight from the statue, no installation. Handles authentication, mounting Core, launcher, DLC.

  • No account, no server
  • Detects the full companion app if present
  • No install needed
On your computer

Forge

Full app installable on desktop. Manages your whole collection: library of all your Relics, DLC, signed updates.

  • Multi-Relic library
  • DLC manager
  • Signed updates
  • Suggested by Ember on first launch
If we closed tomorrow, your Relic would keep working.

That's the promise the rest is built on: the game lives on the object, not on our servers.
